# DFS Group Website — Image Requirements Manifest

This file contains the dimensions, recommended aspect ratios, and crop/subject directions for the placeholder images used across the corporate portal.

---

## 1. Global Image Constraints & Safety Rules

> [!WARNING]
> **Location Compliance**: Do NOT use images showing American highway shields, European winter/snow scenes, or left-side driving lane errors where SADC regional traffic (driving on the left side of the road) is expected.
> **Brand Exclusivity**: Avoid images displaying logos of competitor regional logistics lines.
> **Authenticity**: Avoid AI-generated transport illustrations with unrealistic mechanical configurations.
