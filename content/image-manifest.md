# DFS Group Image Manifest

This manifest outlines the photography requirements and specifications for all visual assets on the DFS Group website.

---

## ⚠️ Compliance & Brand Standards

* **SADC Driving Lanes**: Any road photos showing visible lane directions must depict left-hand driving (standard SADC lane compliance). Avoid right-hand lane captures.
* **Competitor Neutrality**: Ensure all truck trailers and cabs have no visible third-party competitor markings.
* **Mechanical Realism**: Do not use generic AI-generated transport illustrations containing impossible chassis lengths or trailer configurations.
